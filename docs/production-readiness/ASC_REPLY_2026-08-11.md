## 0. Corrections to the 2026-08-06 draft — read before reusing anything

Each of these was verified against the code at HEAD this session. The earlier
draft would have sent Apple three statements the binary does not support, and a
reviewer following it would have looked for a button that is not there.

| The 2026-08-06 draft said | The binary actually does |
|---|---|
| The Discover button reads **"Talk to \<name\>"** | It reads **"Nearby"**, with a video-camera icon (`DiscoverHeroCard.swift:58-61`). A reviewer told to tap "Talk to Solan" will not find it |
| The incoming-call banner shows the caller's **"photo, first name, age and city"** | It shows the caller's **photo, first name, and "Incoming Video Call" / "Incoming Voice Call"**, plus Answer and Decline. **No age, no city** (`IncomingCallBanner.swift:20-52`) |
| The request goes to **that person** | It goes to that person **first**, and simultaneously to up to **7 other** mutually-compatible people who are online at that moment; the first to answer connects (`ring.ts:170-190`, `RING_FANOUT_LIMIT = 8`). If the chosen person is already waiting in the same mode, the two pair immediately with no ring at all (`matchmaking.ts:256-267`) |

Two further facts the draft omitted, both of which a reviewer will hit:

- The banner is up for **7 seconds** client-side; the server accepts for **10**
  (`CallModels.swift:50`, `shared.ts:36`). A reviewer who looks away misses it.
- After tapping the button the caller sees **"Finding someone…"** with no
  identity on screen, for up to 30 seconds, before either connecting or
  reporting "No one's free to talk right now."

## 1. Resolution Center reply — Guideline 2.1

> Thank you for the detailed feedback, and apologies for the time it cost you.
> The fault was ours, not the app's.
>
> **Why you could not reach a match.** Baybe pairs two people only when both
> people's stated preferences accept each other, and a "pass" permanently
> separates a pair. The demo accounts we supplied had been used in our own
> testing until they no longer satisfied those rules, so the account you signed
> into had no eligible partner and could never reach a match. We have reset the
> accounts below and verified end to end that they pair, ring, connect, and
> match. ⚠️
>
> **Baybe needs two people, exactly like a phone call.** With only one account
> signed in the app correctly reports that nobody is available. That is what
> happened during your review.
>
> ---
>
> **1. What a user sees when they are ready to get matched**
>
> The Discover tab shows one specific person, not a queue and not a deck:
> their photo, first name, age, city, and whether they are online now. Around
> them are the photos of other people available right now. The primary button
> below that person starts a live call request to them.
>
> **2. What is shown before a match**
>
> Before any call: photo, first name, age, city, online status. Nothing else
> about a person is available to another user anywhere in Baybe — there is no
> profile browser and no message field. Once the two are connected, the peer's
> name, age, city and occupation are shown full-screen while the call
> establishes, and stay on screen for the whole call alongside a visible
> countdown.
>
> **3. What is shown after a match**
>
> A match exists only after two people have completed a live call together and
> then each *independently and privately* chose "Like" on the post-call screen.
> Only then does a text conversation open. A "Pass" is never revealed to the
> other person. Until that point there is no way for any user to send any
> message to any other user.
>
> **4. How a user sends a video/call request**
>
> On the Discover tab, the button under the person's name and city — labelled
> **"Nearby"**, with a video-camera icon — sends a live call request. It reaches
> the person on the card first, along with a small number of other people who
> are online and mutually compatible at that moment; whoever answers first
> connects. Their app raises an incoming-call banner showing the caller's photo
> and first name, whether it is a video or voice call, and Answer / Decline.
> The banner is live for about 7 seconds. If nobody answers within 30 seconds,
> the caller is told that nobody is free to talk right now.
>

>

>
> - Account A: **+1 650 555 1234**, code **123456**
> - Account B: **+1 650 555 5678**, code **222222**

> **Please sign in on two devices or simulators.** Enter the number on the
> welcome screen (tick the consent box first), then the code on the next screen.
>
> **Exact steps to see a call request sent and answered:**
>
> 1. Sign into Account A on device 1 and Account B on device 2. Allow camera
>    and microphone on both. Leave both on the Discover tab.
> 2. On device 1, Discover shows Account B's person — photo, first name, age,
>    city.
> 3. On device 1, tap the **"Nearby"** button with the video-camera icon,

> 5. Tap **Answer** on device 2. Both devices enter a video call. The peer's
>    name, age and city stay on screen with a countdown.
> 6. Tap **Decline** instead to see the other path: the banner dismisses and
>    the caller keeps waiting for someone else.
> 7. Either side can end the call, or wait for the timer. Both then see a

### Deliberately absent — do not add these back

  nothing backs it. Strike it. Telling App Review something untrue about safety
