Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

Baybe is a dating app built around short video calls between phone-verified adults. Video calls carry both camera and microphone. This policy describes what Baybe collects, how it is used, and the controls you have. It describes only what the app actually does.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: name, date of birth, gender, who you'd like to meet, photos, bio, and interests.
  • Your phone number. Every account verifies one — it keeps the community real. It is used for verification only and is never shown to other users.
  • Approximate location. Two things, both approximate. The city you choose is shown on your profile. Separately, if you allow location access, Baybe keeps a rounded area — your position snapped to a grid of about one kilometre — so it can show you people who are closer to you. Baybe never collects, stores, or shares your precise location, the rounded area is never shown to other users, and declining location access does not limit any feature.
  • Messages you send to your matches, and safety reports you file.

What other users can see

Your name, age, city, gender, who you'd like to meet, and your photos — what your profile card shows. Your date of birth is never shared; only your age is shown. Your phone number is never shown to other users.

How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and profile.
  • To pair you for calls with mutually compatible users and to deliver messages to your matches.
  • To order who you see: among the people you are already compatible with, Baybe ranks those closer to you higher. Distance only changes the order — it never excludes anyone, and people who have not shared a location are ranked normally.
  • To keep the community safe: enforcing our age requirement, acting on reports, and enforcing blocks between users.

Who processes your data

Baybe runs on Google Firebase (Google Cloud): sign-in, profiles, messages, and photos are stored there. Call audio and video pass through LiveKit's servers to connect you, and are never recorded.

What we never do

  • We never record your calls.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never share your date of birth — only your age is shown.
  • We never track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Age requirement

Baybe is for adults 18 and older. Date of birth is required at sign-up and under-18 dates of birth are rejected. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

Deletion and retention

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app. Deletion permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, conversations, and sign-in details. Safety reports filed by or about an account are retained after deletion so that reported behaviour cannot be erased by deleting the account.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, photos, and preferences at any time in the app.
  • Block or report any user; blocks are mutual and permanent.
  • Delete your account in-app (Profile → Delete account), or email us and we will delete it for you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
